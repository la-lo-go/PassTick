PassTick is a fast, free, open source wallet for your tickets.

Keep your boarding passes, event tickets, memberships, and loyalty cards on your phone. Open the app, show the code, and go. No account, no ads, and no subscriptions. It just works at any time!

ORGANIZE YOUR LIFE

- One timeline for everything. Flights, concerts, matches, and hotel nights appear in date order. The agenda calendar shows the days ahead.
- A reminder before each event, with the ticket one tap away.
- Tags for every part of your life: Travel, Work, Family, or your own. Each tag gets a color, so you find any ticket in seconds.
- Pin what matters now, archive what is done, and undo any mistake from the trash.
- Add a date to your calendar and open the location in your map app.
- Search every detail: destination, seat, gate, name, or note.

MAKE IT YOURS

- Your home screen, your way. Show, hide, and reorder the sections you care about.
- Your pass screen, your way. Keep the details you use and hide the rest.
- Your colors. Match your phone, pick an accent, use dark mode, or go full black to save battery.
- A widget with the tickets you choose, right on your home screen.
- Looks great on every screen, from small phones to tablets and foldables.

ALWAYS READY

- Your tickets live on your phone. No login, no connection, no waiting.
- The code shows full screen at maximum brightness, so the scanner reads it on the first try.
- Add tickets in seconds from a file, an email attachment, or the share button.
- Private by default. No account, no ads, no trackers. Nothing is sent anywhere.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE

PassTick is free software under GPL-3.0, and it stays free. It is an independent modern version of the PassAndroid project. You can read the code and help improve it at https://github.com/la-lo-go/PassTick
