First PassTick beta (0.1.1).

- Reads and stores .pkpass and esPass files offline.
- Shows barcodes, pass details, locations, and dates.
- Reminds you before events with notification actions.
- Protects passes with biometrics. Protected content stays out of widgets and the lock screen.
- Tags, pins, archive with undo, and home screen widgets.
- Archive or restore from the swipe actions or the pass menu.
- No account, no ads, no trackers, and no INTERNET permission.
